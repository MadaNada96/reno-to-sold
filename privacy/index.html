1. Who we are

This policy explains how Reno to Sold Inc. ("Reno to Sold", "we", "us"), based in Mississauga, Ontario, handles personal information. It covers renotosold.homes, including the agent and brokerage app, the sign-up page and the homeowner packages page. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

We're accountable for the personal information we control, and we've named a Privacy Officer to oversee this policy. See Contact us below.

2. What we collect

Agents, brokerages and account owners

  • Account details: your name, email, phone number, brokerage, password (stored only in encrypted form), and the photo or logo you upload.
  • Billing details: billing address, billing email, plan, and payment history. Stripe collects your card details. We receive only limited card information, such as the card brand, last four digits and expiry date.
  • Your work: the estimates, package quotes and submissions you create, including the property and client details you enter.
  • Activity: when you sign in, how many estimates you make, and the settings you choose.

Homeowners and sellers

  • When you request a walkthrough on our packages page: your name, email, phone number, property address and home details, your agent's name (if you give it), when you plan to sell, your preferred times, photos you upload, and any notes.
  • When your real estate agent prepares or sends an estimate with us: the details your agent enters, such as your name, contact information and property details.

Contractors and trades

  • If we invite you to bid on work, we collect your name, company, contact details and the bid you submit.

Technical information

  • Your browser's local storage keeps you signed in and saves your work on your device as you go. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.
  • Our hosting providers keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, for security and to keep the Service running.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your account, and to show your branding on the estimates you send.
  • To process your subscription, taxes and invoices, and to send account emails such as activation links, receipts, trial reminders and billing notices.
  • To respond to walkthrough requests and submitted estimates, and to quote, schedule and carry out renovation work.
  • To provide support and answer your questions.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems.
  • To improve our tools and pricing, using summarized or de-identified information where we can.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We only send marketing emails if you've agreed to receive them. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Account and billing emails aren't marketing, and you'll receive them while you have an account.

We don't sell or rent personal information.

4. Consent

We collect, use and share personal information with your consent, or where the law allows us to without it. You give consent when you sign up, submit a form, or ask us to do something for you. You can withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contract requirements. However, we may then be unable to provide some services, for example billing a subscription without a payment method.

Agents: when you enter a client's information or send an estimate to Reno to Sold, you confirm that your client has agreed to you sharing it with us so that we can price and discuss the renovation with them.

5. Who we share it with

  • Your brokerage (if it covers your seat) sees activity only: that you've joined, when you last signed in, and how many estimates you've made. It never sees the content of your estimates.
  • Your clients see what you choose to send them, which includes your name, photo or logo, brokerage and phone number.
  • Your agent: if you're a homeowner and you name your agent or they prepared your estimate, we may keep them informed about your renovation.

  • Business changes: if Reno to Sold is involved in a merger or sale, personal information may be transferred under the same protections.

6. Information stored outside Canada

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Canada, mainly in the United States. While it's there, it's protected by our contracts with those providers, but it may be accessible to courts, law enforcement and national security authorities under the laws of that country. You can contact our Privacy Officer with questions about our providers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Accounts and saved work are kept while your account is open, and after you cancel so you can pick up where you left off if you come back. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.
  • Billing and tax records are kept for at least six years, as Canadian tax law requires.
  • Walkthrough requests and renovation records are kept for as long as we need them to respond, carry out and stand behind the work, and to meet our legal obligations.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. How we protect it

We use safeguards suited to the sensitivity of the information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted password storage, access rules so each person can see only their own data, limited staff access, and payment processing by Stripe, which is certified to industry security standards. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll notify you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, how we've used it, and who we've shared it with;
  • correct information that's inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information, where we don't need to keep it for legal reasons;
  • stop sending you marketing emails.

You can update most account details yourself in the app. For anything else, contact our Privacy Officer. We'll respond within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Children

The Service is for adults working in real estate and for property owners. It isn't intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Service or the law changes. We'll post the new version here with a new "last updated" date, and for important changes, we'll tell account holders by email or in the app.
