Questions homeowners ask

Is the walkthrough really free?

Yes. It's free and there's no obligation. We visit the home, confirm the scope with you and give you your exact price.

Why don't you show prices online?

Every home is different. Ceiling heights, floor condition, rooms to paint and access all change the price, so we confirm it in person. Your quote is in writing before any work starts.

Do I have to list my home with you?

No. You can book any package on its own, and it works with whichever agent you choose. If you renovate and list with Reno to Sold, you also get a discounted renovation, discounted listing fees, and free photography and staging.

Can I stay in my home during the work?

Most clients do. We plan the work around you. You can also hand us the keys, or manage everything from out of town.

Do you do kitchens and bathrooms?

Yes. Package 4 includes the option to renovate the kitchen and bathrooms. We design them with you at the walkthrough.

Which areas do you serve?

Etobicoke, Mississauga, Oakville, Burlington and across the Greater Toronto Area. Not sure if we cover your street? Call 647-500-0270.

What happens to my information?

It goes straight to our team to arrange your walkthrough. We don't sell it or add you to a mailing list.
