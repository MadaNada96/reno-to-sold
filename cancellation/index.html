Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026
Terms of Service Privacy Policy Cancellation & Refunds
The short version
  • Cancel during your 5-day free trial and you pay nothing.
  • Cancel any time after that in one click. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • We don't refund part of a period that has already started, except in the cases listed below.

How to cancel

  1. Sign in at renotosold.homes.
  2. Open Manage billing.
  3. Choose Cancel and confirm.

No phone call or email is needed. If you have trouble, call 647-500-0270 or email renotosold@gmail.com, and we'll cancel it for you from the date you contacted us.

Brokerages: only the account owner can cancel the brokerage plan. Agents covered by a brokerage don't need to cancel anything.

During the free trial

If you cancel before your trial ends, you won't be charged anything. We show your trial end date at sign-up, on the confirmation screen and in Manage billing. If you don't cancel, your plan starts automatically when the trial ends, and your first payment is taken that day.

After you cancel

  • Your plan stays active until the end of the current monthly or annual period, and then stops renewing. You won't be charged again.
  • Your account and saved estimates are kept, so you can restart any time and pick up where you left off. If you'd rather we delete your account, just ask (see our Privacy Policy).
  • For a brokerage, all seats stay active until the end of the period. After that, agents keep their own accounts and saved work, and can continue on their own plan if they wish.

Refunds

Payments for a monthly or annual period that has already started are non-refundable, and we don't give partial refunds for unused time when you cancel. The exceptions are:

  • Billing mistakes: if you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged after you cancelled, we'll refund the incorrect amount in full.
  • Our changes: if we close your account without cause, or remove a core feature you're paying for and you cancel because of it, we'll refund the unused part of your current period.
  • Where the law requires a refund.

To request a refund, email renotosold@gmail.com within 60 days of the charge, with your account email and the invoice date. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Plan changes and credits

  • Switching between monthly and annual is prorated. You only pay the difference for the time that's left.
  • Brokerage seats: adding seats is charged right away for the rest of the period. Reducing seats takes effect right away, and the unused time is credited to your next bill. Credits are applied to future charges and aren't paid out.
  • Joining a brokerage: if you have your own plan and accept a brokerage invitation, your own plan is cancelled and you receive a prorated credit for the unused time. The credit is applied to any future Reno to Sold charge on your account. If you'd prefer it refunded to your card, email us within 60 days.

Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we'll retry it automatically and show a notice in your account so you can update your card. You keep access while we retry. If the payment still can't be collected after several tries, the subscription ends without any further charges. Your account and work are kept.
